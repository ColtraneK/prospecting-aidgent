# Attribution

The evidence-led research approach in `skills/prospecting-aidgent/references/research.md` and related instructions adapts [First Customer Finder](https://github.com/Kappaemme-git/codex-first-customer-finder-skill), by Francesco Mistero. `references/research-framework.md` preserves its detailed research and qualification framework. Its MIT license was inspected on 2026-09-17. This is an independently packaged Aidgentic plugin; it does not install, invoke or claim ownership of that project. It replaces HTML delivery with a maintained Google Sheet and adds locally developed relationship indexing and guarded Sheet helpers.

The relationship parser/helpers also reuse locally developed code from Prospecting Aidgent OS; its Aidgent OS contributors copyright is retained in LICENSE. No private exports or actual prospect records are distributed with this package. Test people and messages are synthetic; template-header tests contain only blank public template structure.
